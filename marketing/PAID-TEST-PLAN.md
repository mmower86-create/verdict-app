## HOW TO READ RESULTS (the actual point)
Watch in Plausible + Meta Ads Manager:
- **Cost per click (CPC):** under ~$1 is good for this audience.
- **Site visits from ads:** should jump to dozens/hundreds (vs your current ~1/day).
- **THE NUMBER THAT MATTERS — signups:** did any ad visitors create an account /
  start a decode? Even 1-2 signups per 100 visitors = the product works, scale it.
  Zero signups from 200+ real visitors = the offer/page needs work, NOT more traffic.

## Decision tree after the test
- Converts well -> pour more budget in, this is your channel. Keep organic as backup.
- Some interest, no signups -> tweak the landing page CTA / offer, retest.
- Total crickets -> bigger rethink on product/audience (better to know now than after months).
