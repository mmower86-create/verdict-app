## WHY THE BRIDGE IS DIFFERENT FROM WHAT WE'VE BEEN DOING
Broadcasting on X = ~6 visitors in 2 weeks. Dead end at this scale.
The bridge is DIRECT and MANUAL: go where renters are ALREADY asking for help,
one human at a time. Lower volume, way higher conversion. This is how you get
your first 10 users, and first 10 is what matters right now.

## AUGUST BRIDGE PLAN (the next 30-60 days, $0)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

### PRIORITY 1 — Facebook groups (best free channel, do this daily)
Lease season is NOW. College move-in is Aug. These groups are peak-active.
- [ ] Join 8-10 groups: "[your city] apartments", "[nearby college] off-campus
      housing", "[city] roommates", "renters rights [state]", "moving to [city]"
- [ ] DAILY: find posts where someone is asking about a lease/deposit/landlord.
      Answer genuinely and specifically. No link. Be THE lease person.
- [ ] When someone's problem is exactly what Verdict solves, then and only then:
      "I actually built a free tool that reads leases for this, getverdict.net"
- Target: 3-5 helpful comments/day. Expect your first real users from here.

### PRIORITY 2 — Direct offer: free lease reviews
The fastest way to a first client is to just DO the thing for people, free.
- [ ] In FB groups / X / Reddit comments: "Happy to run your lease through and
      tell you what's in it, free, just paste it (redact personal info)."
- [ ] Every person who takes you up = a real user, real feedback, possible
      testimonial. Testimonials are what make the paid ads work in September.
- Target: 10 free lease reviews done by end of August.

### PRIORITY 3 — Reddit karma (so it's usable by fall)
Your posts got auto-filtered (account too new). Fix it passively.
- [ ] 2-3 helpful comments/day in r/renters, r/legaladvice, r/personalfinance.
      NO links, no product mentions. Pure karma building.
- By Sept-Oct your account can actually post. Reddit becomes a real channel then.

### PRIORITY 4 — X: maintenance only
- [ ] 1 post/day (Claude posts it). Don't spend more time here. It's brand
      presence, not traffic. Do NOT let this eat the hours that should go to
      Facebook groups and free lease reviews.

### PRIORITY 5 — Record the demo video (do once, 10 min)
- [ ] Screen-record yourself pasting a lease -> verdict appearing.
- Needed for: the September Meta ads (video crushes static), FB group posts,
  and the landing page. Costs 10 minutes, used everywhere.
