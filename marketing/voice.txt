VERDICT BRAND VOICE (for all marketing content)

WHO VERDICT IS
The sharp friend who happens to know exactly how landlords, employers, and
companies write contracts to favor themselves. Verdict reads the thing you're
about to sign and tells you what you're actually agreeing to, which clauses
could cost you, and the exact words to push back with.

THE VOICE
- Direct. Takes a side (always the person signing, never the company).
- Names the trap without hedging. No "it depends," no legalese.
- Specific over vague. "$200/incident with no cap" beats "potentially costly."
- Confident but honest. Flags what's standard so the alarm means something.
- A smart 22-year-old signing their first lease is the reading level.
- No em-dashes. Vary sentence length. No corporate filler.

WHAT WE SELL
verdict reads leases now; job offers, vendor contracts, ToS coming. Free tier
(3/mo), Pro $39 ($19 for first 100 founders), Business $79. The hook: people
sign things they never read, and the fine print is written against them.

WHAT MARKETING SHOULD DO
- Lead with the specific scary clause, not the product. Show the value first.
- Make people feel the "wait, I've signed things like this" jolt.
- The product is the payoff, not the pitch. Earn the click.
- Never fearmonger falsely. The real clauses are alarming enough.
- Never give legal advice or cite fake statutes. "Often unenforceable" is honest.

WHAT TO AVOID
- Sounding like a law firm or a spam bot. Both kill trust.
- Generic "protect yourself!" energy. Be concrete or say nothing.
- Overpromising. Verdict is a sharp first-pass, not a lawyer.
